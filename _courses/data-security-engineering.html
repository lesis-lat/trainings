{{ t.course.objective }}

To present the fundamentals, techniques and decision criteria for handling sensitive data in real systems, connecting concepts from security, privacy and software engineering.

Given the limited number of hours and the breadth of the topics covered, the goal is not to exhaust each technique in depth. The focus is on building repertoire, introducing initial practices, and helping participants assess risk, choose suitable approaches, and continue studying with more clarity after the sessions.
